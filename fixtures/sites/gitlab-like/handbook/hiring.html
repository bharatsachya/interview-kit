How we hire

Every engineering candidate goes through the same four stages. We do not do whiteboard algorithm puzzles.

  1. Screening call — 30 minutes with a recruiter.
  2. Take-home exercise — a small, self-contained problem. We budget four hours and pay for your time.
  3. System design interview — 60 minutes. You will be asked to design an ingest pipeline under a stated throughput budget, and to defend the trade-offs.
  4. Values conversation — 45 minutes with two engineers from other teams.

We give a decision within five working days of the final stage. Candidates who ask good questions about our failure modes tend to do well.
